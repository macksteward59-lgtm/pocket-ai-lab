# Frozen public-document reader validation

The #287 reader selects one sentence or abstains. This evaluation moves from two-sentence summaries to the complete supplied abstract and section paragraphs of QASPER papers. Figures, table images and reference metadata are outside the reader input. QASPER's questions and reference annotations were authored independently of Pocket AI Lab. Sources: [Dasigi et al.](https://aclanthology.org/2021.naacl-main.365/) and the [QASPER dataset and attribution](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/qasper).

## Frozen protocol

Use QASPER 0.3 test distribution SHA-256 `72a52a41193e2838b8074f80ac074b94f956b84886c36a61c58a7df4171bdd72`. The hard and soft models are byte-for-byte exports from [the first pilot](https://github.com/macksteward59-lgtm/pocket-ai-lab/tree/5233a79a216c1b46896decdb5508377079cdc921/coordination/evidence/public-source-distillation-2026-11-22). Freeze both decision thresholds at 0.5 and the word-overlap threshold at 0.15. Fit no weights and select no threshold on the new collection.

Consider papers whose titles mention translation, summarization, parsing, morphology, coreference, captioning, dialogue, or speech recognition. Limit the slice to general language research, excluding offensive-security topics. Require at least 40 sentence candidates. Keep complete nonempty text paragraphs from the abstract and body, in order, and split on whitespace following a sentence-ending punctuation mark. Preserve paragraph boundaries. Evaluate up to 24 supported questions and 24 unanimously unanswerable questions in stable SHA-256 order of the question ID, with at most two questions per paper in each group. Report the actual available and selected counts. The sample is balanced by design and cannot estimate prevalence.

A supported question requires no unanswerable annotation and at least one reference with a single extractive answer that occurs in a candidate sentence within that reference's evidence paragraph. For these questions a correct selection must satisfy both conditions for the same reference. All annotations must mark an unanswerable question unanswerable. Exclude disagreements, absent evidence alignments, multi-span-only and generative-only answers. Keep incorrect selections separate from refusals. Also score the supported questions with only their annotated evidence paragraphs available, as a diagnostic upper-context advantage.

Evaluate the frozen hard reader, soft reader and fixed overlap baseline on the exact same inputs. Record choices and hashes, count every failure, and retain per-question reference indices. Compare native hard-reader choices and every sentence score against the Python reference with absolute tolerance 1e-8. Document accuracy and platform parity independently. Keep the reader experimental if it gives unsupported answers. iOS simulator execution and physical iPhone Air validation are separate milestones.
